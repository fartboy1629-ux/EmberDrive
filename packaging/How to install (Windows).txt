EMBER DRIVE - HOW TO INSTALL (Windows)

1. Copy the "Ember Drive.vst3" folder into:

       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3

   Windows will ask for administrator permission. Click Continue.

2. Open your music software and rescan your plug-ins.

3. Load "Ember Drive" (maker: Ember Audio) on a track as an effect.
   It's listed under Distortion.

If it doesn't show up: right-click the zip file you downloaded, choose
Properties, tick "Unblock", click OK, then unzip and copy again.
